# Maintainers

| GitHub | Role | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| [@moosunny](https://github.com/moosunny) | Maintainer | Dataset content, SAST re-verification, releases |
| [@Sparrow-Co-Ltd](https://github.com/Sparrow-Co-Ltd) | Company account of Sparrow Co., Ltd. | Repository administration and settings |

Maintainers review issues and pull requests, decide on dataset changes, and publish releases as
described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

Maintainers are also the community leaders responsible for enforcing the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). To report a conduct concern, follow
[How to report](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#how-to-report).
